## Definition of Done

An issue is Done only when its acceptance criteria are met, meaningful tests pass, relevant documentation/models are updated, a different team member reviews the PR, corrections are resolved, and the PR is merged. An assistant-generated package is a review input, not evidence that the team completed two Sprints.

## Workflow

Todo -> In Progress -> In Review -> Done.
Use an actual issue-linked branch. Reference actual issue numbers in commits and PRs. Each student uses their own account. Never fabricate commits, reviews or attendance on another person's behalf.

## Evidence during each Sprint

Planning date and Sprint Goal; prioritized backlog; dated progress changes; meaningful reviewed PRs; demo/review outcomes; retrospective improvement with owner and target date; actual contribution links. Record a short coordination note when the team meets; the assignment does not prescribe a fabricated daily meeting schedule.

## Review versus retrospective

Review: what increment worked, what was accepted, feedback and remaining work.
Retrospective: how the team worked, one specific problem, corrective action, owner and target date.
